## 5. INV-20 triangulation — pointers, not prose

- **Empty rooms.** `uofa:Requirement`, `uofa:AssuranceClaim`,
  `uofa:OperatingEnvelope`, `uofa:ApplicabilityConstraint`: declared classes,
  **0** declared properties each.
- **Undeclared terms in use.** `specification`, `acceptanceThreshold`,
  `comparisonValue`, `comparisonMetric`, `quantityOfInterest`, `passed` — used in
  the corpus, declared in neither vocabulary nor context.
- **Optional structure goes unpopulated.** `requiredVerificationMethod` is
  declared and rule-read, populated in **1 of 78** packages.
- **No inference element.** `factorConstraintWarrants` is `owl:deprecated`, its
  comment reading *"not used by any package or shape"*.
- **Worked example.** Row 16, `adv-2026-p2-119-confusion-necessary-sufficient_high-v03`
  — V&V 40 compliant, every structural fact correct, and the decision record
  argues a necessary condition is sufficient. Adjudicating it required a human to
  read a paragraph and name a fallacy.
